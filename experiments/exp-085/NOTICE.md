# Source and attribution

The Invsqrt generator is from team ThemBones' public Kaggle dataset
dziyanavalenta/q2026-v155-isr-generator, licensed Apache-2.0. The upstream
LICENSE, NOTICE, CREDITS, and third-party license notices are preserved in
upstream/licenses.

The upstream notice credits the generator's lineage to Syed Asad Ali's
Apache-2.0 Invsqrt generator, plus public optimization ideas and tools listed
in NOTICE_generators. This experiment runs the pinned source builder inside a
private Kaggle kernel and records the exact generated circuit hash. It does
not submit a compiled circuit downloaded from the source dataset.

The Kaggle kernel wrapper, hash checks, environment guard, and archive
integration are this project's work. The portfolio also contains exp-063's
source-built DQI and exp-074's source-built V20 chemistry; their attribution
and verifier receipts remain in their respective experiment records.
